DirtyHands creates custom ceramic panels and art objects based on original sketches and technical specifications. We work with projects of any complexity, carefully immersing ourselves in the concept and architecture of the space.

We're open to collaboration with designers, decorators and architects. Together we create unique ceramic solutions for interiors and exteriors: entrances, facades, private and public spaces.

If your project needs more than decor, an expressive artistic accent, we'd love to hear from you.
