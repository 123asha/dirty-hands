Clay with something to say

At DirtyHands, we create original design pieces — from tabletop art objects and wall panels to sculptural forms and interior accents that enhance spaces and set their emotional tone.

The studio was founded by designers and entrepreneurs Lisa and Karen, who combine professional expertise with artistic intuition to bring unconventional ideas to life. Their approach reimagines ceramics, moving beyond functional plates and pots towards an artistic language that expresses the style, mood, and character of a space.
